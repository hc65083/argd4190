Steps

1

Click or hover on Tests in the menu nav. Here, you can take a test that matches you with a blind box character. Select the options that resonate with you the most, and make sure to hit submit after the test to see your results!

2

Once you get your character, navigate to Sets to learn more about them.

3

Click Print in the menu nav. You can browse and click on the one you want to print.

4

That's it! 3D prints can take a long time but it will be worth it :D
