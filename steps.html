Steps

1

Click on Design in the menu nav. Here, you can customize your blind box by selecting different options. Make sure to hit save after every change!

2

Once you save your design, it should be saved to your account (Click Accounts to view or create an account).

3

Click on Print in the menu nav. You should see your saved designs and click on the one you want to print.

4

You're almost done! 3D prints can take a long time but it will be worth it :D
